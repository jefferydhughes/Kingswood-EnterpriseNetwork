How Pathway 01 works

The course teaches the business. The brand does the heavy lifting. Nobody starts from a blank page on services, pricing, or marketing.

1

Take the course

Business foundations: self-assessment, customers, finances, marketing, and launch.

2

Choose a brand

Pick the concept that fits your gifts, your town, and your schedule.

3

Train in Sussex

About two weeks of hands-on training on campus, on real jobs where possible.

4

Launch at home

Open under a recognizable brand, from home, a vehicle, or your church.

5

Stay supported

A small monthly fee covers booking, marketing, coaching, and a peer network.

The brands are designed to complement each other, so an operator can grow by adding a partner brand instead of starting over.

Plumb Line+Second Stone

Visits and errands during the week, plus a social day hosted in the church building.
